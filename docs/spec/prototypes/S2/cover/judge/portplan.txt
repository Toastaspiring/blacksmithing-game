===== risks
1. Sealed-round-filler channel. Any inscribed-polygon model keeps a truly hidden round filler sealed by flat walls at a gap within one sagitta below HIDE_OFFSET (tier flips, |dD| 0.47, valid designs). The sagitta is 0.0048·r at 32 sides and 0.057·r for the n=4 Ball. More sides narrow the window (r 0.2: 0.00096 at 32 sides, 0.00024 at 64, 0.00006 at 128) but never close it. The model layout is deterministic, so the window can be targeted.

2. A model-error band around 2 % on round parts: false Enclosed up to 3.53 % (base), 2.95 % (canon), 2.82 % (fine); false kept down to 1.29 %; effect on D ≤ 0.0264.

3. Cost without a cap: about 0.9 s Studio per verdict for a design that passes V1–V3, and V5-invalid designs still pay it.

4. Many small tables are allocated; GC pressure and checkpoint gaps are unverified on an idle machine.

5. Units overstate the separating-axis pre-check. Fix the counting before freezing a cap value.

6. A design right at the cap could be valid while its mirror image is not, since units differ slightly between them.

7. The Ball model is 95.07 % of the sphere's area, and shares are taken on the model's area.

8. Lune 0.8.9's serde JSON is lossy. The encoder writes |x| below about 1e-16 (and −0, and subnormals) as 0; the decoder misparses some 17-digit values. So worst_P.json, the verifiers' JSON designs and my climbs are not bit-exact copies of what was measured.
